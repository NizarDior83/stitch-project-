Lumis is a puzzle about lighting a reef, one crystal at a time. No timer. No score. Nothing you can lose.

Move across the water Touch the water

Nothing here can be failed.

There is no clock and no score. A level ends when the reef is lit, whether that takes you forty seconds or four nights. Put it down mid-puzzle and it waits exactly where you left it.

One crystal lights its neighbours at half.

Touch a crystal and it takes full light. The six around it take half. The ring beyond takes a quarter. Every puzzle in the game is that one rule, folded into a shape you have not seen before.

Below four hundred metres, the reef makes its own light.

Deep Reef is the second world. Same rule, no sunlight — every glow down here came from a crystal you lit yourself. It unlocks once and stays unlocked.
